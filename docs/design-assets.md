# Графика сайта

Знак и иллюстрации созданы встроенным GPT Image. В приложении иллюстрации хранятся в WebP, знак — в PNG с прозрачностью.

## Знак

[Файл изображения](../public/team-brand.png)

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: the standalone logo mark for a Russian employee activity and rewards site named Командный ход. Generate exactly one tightly composed, flat brand symbol on a genuinely transparent square background. The mark should be a compact silhouette of two chunky, opposing paper-tab forms that share a central notch, read as two teammates passing a game piece, and have a subtle Cyrillic К silhouette without literally typesetting a letter. Make it energetic and asymmetric: one deep aubergine #28233b form, one warm vermilion #de4f37 form, with a confident ivory negative-space gap. Two solid inks, clean rounded-cut geometry, clear at 28 pixels, optically balanced, 13% transparent padding on every side. Art direction: graphic identity by a contemporary independent print studio. No wordmark, typography, emblem border, gradient, shadow, grain, outlined forms, hands, arrows, gloss, extra concepts, mockups, or checkered backdrop.

## Главная

[Файл изображения](../public/team-move-hero.webp)

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: hero photograph for Командный ход, a warm, editorial employee challenges and rewards website. Create a wide landscape, art-directed overhead studio photograph of one cooperative move on a custom analog tabletop game. A large ivory game sheet with thin aubergine rectangular route lines sits on a uniform golden marigold tabletop (#efd17e); two real hands enter neatly from opposite edges, one moving a substantial vermilion wooden playing pawn, the other steadying a single deep aubergine piece. A couple of orderly printed geometric cells are enough. Crop on the hands at the wrist so they look intentional. The image reads instantly at a 640px width; the central action is fully readable in a central 70% crop. Clear directional late-morning shadows, visible paper fibers and painted wood, rich familiar print colors and natural skin tone. Elevated, quiet, playful editorial product photography, not corporate stock. One composition fills the canvas with no margin. No lettering, text, numbers, logos, arrows, overlaid UI, phones, gradients, scattered confetti, border frame or stock-photo smiles.

## Каталог наград

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: landscape editorial accent photo for the rewards section of a site for employee challenges. Art-directed still life of a real warm-ivory ceramic espresso mug with espresso and a red paper claim ticket with absolutely no writing, sitting on a golden yellow tabletop. Include a neatly folded piece of ivory paper and a single dark aubergine wooden game token as secondary objects. Three-quarter top-down view with objects grouped near the middle and clear margins, compose for a wide horizontal crop. The item surfaces are matte, the shadows are tactile and directional, and the color story is marigold, vermilion, aubergine, and ivory. Evoke the independent print/design shop physicality and a small workplace reward. No text, no numbers, no brand, no graphic overlay, no gradient, no collage seam, no plastic or neon.
